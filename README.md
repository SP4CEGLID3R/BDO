# BDO EU Craft Planner

A single-page calculator that ranks Black Desert Online EU crafts (processing, alchemy, cooking, cups and more) by silver per hour after market tax.

- Live EU prices are fetched in your browser from the community market API at [arsha.io](https://api.arsha.io). No account or backend needed.
- Prices refresh automatically when they are over 30 minutes old, or with the **Refresh prices** button.
- Set your Value Pack, Family Fame, Processing Mastery and processing speed; edit any price, material amount or output amount.
- Settings and edits are stored in your own browser only.

Recipes and average yields come from BDO Codex. Crafts per mass process by mastery come from Grumpy Green's processing mastery table.
