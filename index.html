Cost is the price to craft once, Sells for is one craft at the cheapest current listing before tax, and Profit is after tax. Click a craft to see every material and its price. * means nothing is listed right now, so the market base price is used.

Your price replaces the market price everywhere the item appears, as a material and as a product. Leave it blank to follow the live market.

Live prices are the cheapest EU listing and top buy order for each item, read straight from the community market API (arsha.io) by your browser. Prices refresh by themselves when they are over 30 minutes old, or when you press Refresh prices. Units sold per hour start from a snapshot taken on 14 Nov 2026 and then follow the change in each item's sales counter between your refreshes, averaged with the previous rate, so items that sell rarely are noisy. Recipes and average yields are from BDO Codex with no mastery bonus. Crafts per mass process by mastery are from Grumpy Green's mastery table. The Rich Merchant's Ring bonus comes from a 2020 patch note and has not been confirmed for today.

Your settings, price edits, recipe edits and last fetched prices are kept in this browser only. Nothing is sent anywhere except the price requests to arsha.io.
