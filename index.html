Profit per hour counts only what actually sells within your waiting time, divided by the hours you craft. Listings at the same price sell in the order they were listed. By default you list one price step below the cheapest listing so yours sells first; at the market's minimum price you can't, so you wait for the listings ahead of you. Sales per hour are averages over the last (up to 7 days, from readings saved in this browser).

The plan looks for the mix of crafts that makes the most total profit within your silver and crafting time. Each craft is capped at the share of its market you expect to win in the selling time, at the materials currently listed, and at your crafting time. Routes that make the same item share one market. Material costs follow the real listings, so prices rise as the plan buys more, and it stops adding crafts once the next batch would no longer pay. Listings for the plan's materials load in a few seconds.
